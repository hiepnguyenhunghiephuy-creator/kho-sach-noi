Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Tortoise and the Hare. Let's begin!

Boss: Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a hare and a tortoise. The hare was very, very fast. He could run like the wind. The tortoise was very, very slow. He walked step by step, slowly, slowly.

Boss: One sunny morning, the hare saw the tortoise walking on the road. The hare laughed and laughed. Ha ha ha! You are so slow! You will never win a race! The tortoise was not angry. He smiled and said, Let's have a race. The hare laughed again. You? Race with me? Okay! Let's do it!

Boss: All the animals in the forest came to watch. The fox was the judge. He said, Ready? Go! The hare ran very fast. Zoom! In one minute, he was far, far away. He looked back. The tortoise was so far behind. The hare thought, I am too fast. I can take a nap. The tortoise will never catch me.

Boss: So the hare lay down under a big tree and fell asleep. Zzz. Meanwhile, the tortoise walked on. Step, step, step. Slowly, slowly, but he never stopped. He walked past the sleeping hare. Step, step, step. He was getting closer to the finish line.

Boss: At last, the hare woke up. Oh no! Where is the tortoise? He ran as fast as he could to the finish line. But it was too late! The tortoise was already there, smiling. All the animals cheered. Hooray for the tortoise!

Boss: The hare learned an important lesson that day. Being fast is good, but never giving up is better.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Slow and steady wins the race. Thank you for listening, my friends! See you in the next story. Goodbye!
