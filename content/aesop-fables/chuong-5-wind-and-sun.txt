Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome back to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Wind and the Sun. Let's begin!

Boss: High up in the sky, the Wind and the Sun were having an argument. I am stronger than you! said the Wind, blowing hard. Whoosh! No, I am stronger than you! said the Sun, shining brightly.

Boss: Just then, they saw a man walking on the road below. He was wearing a warm coat. Let's have a contest, said the Wind. The one who can make the man take off his coat is the strongest!

Boss: The Wind went first. He blew and blew with all his power. Whoosh! Whoosh! The trees bent down. Leaves flew everywhere. But the man felt the cold wind, so he held his coat tighter and tighter. He even buttoned it up! The harder the Wind blew, the tighter the man held his coat.

Boss: At last, the Wind was tired. He could blow no more. Now it is my turn, said the Sun with a warm smile. The Sun began to shine softly. Warm, golden light covered the road. It felt so nice and warm.

Boss: The man smiled. Ah, what a beautiful day! He wiped his forehead. It was getting warm. So he unbuttoned his coat. Then, as the Sun shone even warmer, the man took off his coat completely and sat down happily in the sunshine.

Boss: The Wind had to agree. You win, dear Sun. You are stronger than me. The Sun smiled kindly and said, Being gentle is often stronger than being rough.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Gentleness and kindness are stronger than force. Thank you for listening, my friends! See you in the next story. Goodbye!
