Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome back to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Ant and the Grasshopper. Let's begin!

Boss: It was summer. The sun was warm and bright. In a green field, a grasshopper was singing and dancing all day long. La la la! Life is beautiful! He played his music and jumped happily in the grass.

Boss: Near him, a little ant was working very hard. She carried a big grain of wheat on her back. Step by step, she walked to her home under the ground. Then she went back for more. And more. And more.

Boss: The grasshopper laughed at the ant. Why do you work so hard? Come and sing with me! The sun is shining! But the ant said, Winter will come soon. I must save food for the cold days. The grasshopper just laughed. Winter? That is far away! Let's have fun!

Boss: Day after day, the ant worked hard, and the grasshopper played. Soon, the leaves turned yellow and red. The air became cold. Winter had come! Snow covered the field. Brrr! It was very, very cold.

Boss: The grasshopper was hungry. He had no food at all. His stomach made funny sounds. He remembered the ant and knocked on her door. Please, dear ant, can you share some food with me? I am so hungry!

Boss: The kind ant opened the door. She gave him warm soup and bread. As the grasshopper ate, he understood his mistake. I played all summer, and you worked. Now I know: we must prepare for the future.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Work hard today, and you will be happy tomorrow. Thank you for listening, my friends! See you in the next story. Goodbye!
