Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome back to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs. It is our last story in this collection. Let's begin!

Boss: Once upon a time, there was a poor farmer. He lived in a small house with his wife. They had a little farm with one special goose. Every morning, the farmer went to the goose's nest, hoping to find an egg.

Boss: One wonderful morning, the farmer found something amazing in the nest. It was an egg, but not a normal egg. It was bright, shiny, and made of pure gold! Wow! The farmer could not believe his eyes. He ran to the town and sold the golden egg for a lot of money.

Boss: The next morning, there was another golden egg in the nest! And the next morning, another one! Every day, one golden egg. Soon, the farmer and his wife became rich. They bought a big house, fine clothes, and delicious food.

Boss: But the farmer was not happy. He became greedy. He thought, If I get only one egg a day, I must wait a long time to be very, very rich. If I cut open the goose, I can take all the golden eggs inside at once!

Boss: So, with a heavy heart, the farmer did a terrible thing. But when he looked inside the goose, there were no golden eggs at all! It was just a normal goose inside. The farmer cried, but it was too late. No more golden eggs, ever again.

Boss: The farmer and his wife learned a painful lesson. Because of greed, they lost everything they had.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Be happy with what you have. Greed can make you lose everything. Thank you for listening to all six stories, my dear friends! Keep being kind, honest, and hardworking. Goodbye, and see you soon!
