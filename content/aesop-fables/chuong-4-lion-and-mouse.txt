Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome back to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Lion and the Mouse. Let's begin!

Boss: In a big, wild forest, there lived a strong lion. He was the king of all animals. His roar was so loud that the trees shook. Roar! All the animals were afraid of him.

Boss: One sunny afternoon, the lion was sleeping under a tree. A tiny mouse was running and playing nearby. By accident, the little mouse ran right over the lion's big nose! The lion woke up at once. He caught the mouse under his huge paw.

Boss: Please, please, don't eat me! cried the little mouse. I am so small. One day, I will help you! The lion laughed a big, loud laugh. Ha ha ha! You? Help me? You are so tiny! But the lion was in a good mood, so he opened his paw and let the mouse go. Thank you, thank you! said the mouse, and ran away quickly.

Boss: Many days later, the lion was walking in the forest when, snap! He stepped into a hunter's net! The more he moved, the tighter the net became. He roared and roared, but no animal could help him.

Boss: Then he heard a small voice. Don't worry, my friend! I am here! It was the little mouse! She climbed up the net and began to chew the ropes with her sharp little teeth. Chew, chew, chew! Soon, there was a big hole in the net. The lion was free!

Boss: The lion looked at the little mouse with kind eyes. Thank you, little friend. You were right. Even the smallest friend can be the greatest helper.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Kindness is never wasted. No act of kindness, however small, is ever a waste. Thank you for listening, my friends! See you in the next story. Goodbye!
