Boss: Hello, my young friends! Welcome back to Aesop's Fables for Kids. I am Boss, your storyteller. Today's story is called The Boy Who Cried Wolf. Let's begin!

Boss: Long, long ago, in a small village near the mountains, there lived a young shepherd boy. Every day, he took his sheep up the green hill to eat fresh grass. But the boy was often bored. There was nothing to do but watch the sheep. Bah, bah, said the sheep.

Boss: One day, the boy had a naughty idea. He wanted some fun. So he ran down the hill to the village and shouted, Wolf! Wolf! A big wolf is eating my sheep! Help! Help!

Boss: The kind villagers grabbed their sticks and ran up the hill as fast as they could. But when they got there, there was no wolf. The boy was laughing. Ha ha! There is no wolf! I was only joking! The villagers were angry, but they went back home.

Boss: A few days later, the boy did it again. Wolf! Wolf! Help! The villagers ran up the hill again. And again, there was no wolf. The boy laughed even louder. You are so funny, they said, shaking their heads.

Boss: Then, one dark evening, a real wolf came. It was big and hungry, with sharp teeth. The boy was very scared. He shouted with all his voice, Wolf! Wolf! A real wolf! Please help me!

Boss: But this time, no one came. The villagers heard him, but they thought, He is joking again. So they stayed in their warm houses. The wolf scared all the sheep away, and the poor boy cried.

Boss: The boy learned a very sad lesson that day. When you lie many times, people will not believe you, even when you tell the truth.

Boss: The moral of our story is: Always tell the truth. Thank you for listening, my friends! See you in the next story. Goodbye!
